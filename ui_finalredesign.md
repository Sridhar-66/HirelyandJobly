Design system prompt: "AccioJob-style" edtech/placement landing page

Visual style:

Clean, modern SaaS look — white background, generous whitespace, rounded corners (12–16px) on cards
Primary accent: bold blue/indigo for CTAs and highlights; soft blue/purple gradient decorative blobs behind hero and section cards
Typography: bold, large headlines (36–48px) with a mixed-weight highlight word in a different color; body text in muted gray
Buttons: solid rounded-pill CTAs ("Book Free Counselling", "View Courses") with high contrast, plus a lighter secondary "Download Brochure" style button

Layout patterns:

Sticky top navbar: logo left, nav links center, primary CTA button right
Hero: large headline + subtext + trust badges row (recognitions/press logos) underneath
Two-tile feature cards (like "Courses" / "Jobs") — icon-style illustration, short pitch, single CTA link
Stats bar: 4 big numbers with small labels underneath (e.g., "2000+ STUDENTS PLACED")
Tabbed course/product cards: tab switcher (Online/Offline/Self-Paced) above a horizontal scroll of cards, each with duration badge, tech-stack tag pills, and a "scholarship available" ribbon
Location tabs with a stat-cards + testimonial layout per location
Alumni/testimonial carousel: swipeable cards with photo, name, role, company logo, package, and a short quote
FAQ accordion grouped under sub-tabs (Courses / Learning / Placements)
Footer: dark or light multi-column link grid + social icons + contact block + trust badges repeated

Interaction details:

Subtle hover lift on cards
Tab-based filtering instead of separate pages for related content
Sticky WhatsApp floating button bottom-right
